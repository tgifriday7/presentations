Point of view

Running an Americas pre-sales organisation

Ten ideas, each with a claim, three proofs, one measure and the question that would test it at Ping. Everything traces to work I have done. Nothing here is a finding about Ping; it is what I would want to validate together.

Brandon Freitag  ·  September 2026

What the seat is for  ·  1 of 10

Four numbers: three the business already tracks, and the one that decides retention.

Technical win rate, competitive new logo
Against Okta and Entra, by segment and by competitor. Where the moat holds: regulated, hybrid, sovereign, complex customer identity.
POV conversion, migration and attach
Self-managed to SaaS, ForgeRock to AIC, and the attach of Protect, Verify, Governance, Privilege and Identity for AI. Two thirds of bookings live here.
Cost per technical win
The number the business prices. Capacity comes from qualification, spans, partners and automation before it comes from headcount.
What happened next
Adoption against what was sold, outcome achieved, expansion and retention, cut by pre-sale team. The number a signature never proves.
The measure
Whether the four exist in the CRM today, and what moved in two quarters. Start with how often the business-outcome field is filled in.
The question that tests it here
Which of these four does the forecast read today, and who sees post-close outcomes by pre-sale team?
Business-outcome selling  ·  2 of 10

Pursuits anchored to a customer outcome win at a multiple of product-led ones.

The portfolio told me where to look
At Google Cloud one class of pursuit was about 20% of the work and 70% of the revenue. I proposed and founded a practice on that evidence.
Measured before scaling
Win-rate research across engagement motions: outcome-anchored pursuits won at roughly 9x the rate of RFI-driven ones.
Sustained, not a pilot
Qualification rebuilt around the finding, which meant declining work. The practice held 2x the field win rate against a $10.98B North America target.
The measure
Win rate by engagement motion and by competitor, tracked quarterly.
The question that tests it here
Where does the field lose deals the platform should be winning?
Up-levelling an organisation to it  ·  3 of 10

Enablement does not beat an incentive. Change the gate and the comp, then train.

Reach I can count
A 180-plus principal architect community engaged and 300-plus infrastructure specialists enabled on the method.
Adoption stayed the exception
The people who ran outcome-based engagements did well and stayed the minority, because the incentive was to push product.
The fix is structural
A qualification gate agreed with sales and written into the cadence; variable comp tied to technical wins and POV conversion; certification with teeth.
The measure
Share of pursuits that clear the gate, and POV conversion before and after.
The question that tests it here
Where do Sales and SE incentives point in different directions today?
Sales partnership  ·  4 of 10

Co-pilot means a shared standard and a shared number.

Decided together, in advance
The qualification standard is agreed between the two leaders and lives in the cadence, so no deal becomes SE versus rep.
The right to decline, exercised rarely
Time-boxed POCs with success criteria are how SE says yes without losing the quarter. Declining is the exception the standard makes possible.
SE carries a number next to sales
Technical win rate and POV conversion sit beside bookings in the same review. I own mine the way the sales leader owns theirs.
The measure
Technical win probability by stage, readable in the forecast.
The question that tests it here
What does SE owe your forecast today, and what would you want it to owe?
Metrics and rhythms  ·  5 of 10

Outcomes on the scorecard, activity as diagnostics, one cadence that cascades.

Four outcome numbers
Technical win rate by motion and competitor; POV conversion and cycle time; cost per technical win; what happened after the signature.
Activity as inputs, never as the score
Demos, workshops and POCs are diagnostics of where hours go. They explain the outcome numbers; they do not replace them. Deals qualified out or scoped down are recorded as decisions with a reason, not as losses.
A weekly rhythm about deals and people
Deal and POC reviews, a planning artifact that cascades from the SVP's, and a quarterly review the sales leader sits in.
The measure
Cycle time at evaluation stage, and forecast accuracy where SE input feeds it.
The question that tests it here
Where does forecast accuracy break, by stage or by segment?
Capacity without headcount  ·  6 of 10

There are four sources of capacity, and headcount is not the first.

Qualification, fastest
Declining pursuits that win at a ninth of the rate frees more hours than any tool, and shows up in a quarter.
Spans and the senior IC track
Player-coach leaders returning to full contribution through Principal and Distinguished roles restores hours without a req, and keeps the people.
Partners, then automation
Integrators delivering migrations so SE hours go to winning. Then the grind automated: RFx, POC environments, discovery synthesis. The SE organisation running its own agents under the product is the demo.
The measure
Hours freed per SE per quarter, and where they went: to executive discovery, readiness and the honest diagnosis, or to more demos.
The question that tests it here
How much of the leaders' time is player-coach today, and how is it counted?
Two plays, two scorecards  ·  7 of 10

Migration and attach inside the base and competitive new logo are different motions.

The base is most of the business
A mature identity vendor books most new ACV from existing customers: migration to SaaS, ForgeRock to AIC, attach of new services. The risk is destabilising a SAML estate; the reward is retention.
New logo runs into the bundle
Entra is already in the E5 contract. You do not beat a bundle on features; you win the use cases it cannot serve and quantify the cost of the gap.
One win rate hides which is broken
Two plays, two scorecards, two kinds of SE hour. Read separately or you fix the wrong one.
The measure
Win rate and POV conversion by play, not blended.
The question that tests it here
What is the split of the Americas number between the base and net-new, and where is the win rate weakest?
The agentic motion  ·  8 of 10

A specialist pod that graduates, incubated in the region that has the references.

Shape
An overlay of four to six from the senior IC track, one of whom speaks agent-builder language: MCP, token exchange, agent frameworks. The buyer is platform engineering and the CISO, not the IAM team.
Mandate
A fixed-deliverable discovery workshop that scores whether the organisation can absorb what is built, not only whether it works; eight to twelve lighthouse POVs, each one real agent use case with an auditable success criterion; turn what works into a play and certify generalists.
Graduation
The pod's metric is the share of agentic deals run without it, with a named steward measured on twelve-month outcomes. Incubated in AMER, handed to the global function when the play and the people are ready.
The measure
Referenceable wins with a named buyer; share of agentic deals run without the pod.
The question that tests it here
Do specialists sit in the regions or globally today, and where should the agentic motion live?
The first two quarters  ·  9 of 10

Diagnose, instrument, then move. Everything before the data is a hypothesis.

Days 1 to 30: listen
One-to-ones with every leader, ride-alongs on the top pursuits, deal and POC reviews to see where technical wins are actually lost. Two cheap tests: how often the business-outcome field in the CRM is filled in, and whether leaders across functions describe what the SE organisation is for the same way.
Days 31 to 60: instrument and agree
The four numbers live in the CRM. The qualification standard agreed with the sales leader. What is funded and the checkpoints agreed with leadership, in writing. The pod named from existing talent; the first lighthouses chosen.
Days 61 to 90: first results
First POV outcomes on the new standard, first certification wave, and a proposal on spans through the senior IC track. Then the second quarter is execution.
The measure
What changed by the end of the second quarter, in the four numbers, against checkpoints agreed in the first sixty days.
The question that tests it here
What would make you say this hire was a great decision at twelve months?
Where this comes from  ·  10 of 10

Built, sold, sat on the customer side, and measured all of it.

Built and ran the practice
Proposed and founded a strategic architecture practice at Google Cloud; pursuits of $20M-plus, many above $100M, the largest in the billions.
Ran a large organisation inside a regulated enterprise
140 people through two layers of managers at a Fortune 200 healthcare system, across the CIO, the CISO and business leadership, holding HIPAA commitments while cutting operating cost 27%.
Measures before trusting the assumption
A solo patent on the process I was measuring at Motorola; a Six Sigma black belt; the same instinct produced the 9x finding twenty-five years later.
The measure
The four numbers on page one, two quarters in.
The question that tests it here
Is there anything about my background that makes you uncertain about the fit?
Measure first.
Then move.

Every page here is a hypothesis until the data says otherwise.
